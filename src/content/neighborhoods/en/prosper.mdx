## Who Prosper fits

Prosper is the move-up destination. It sits at the northern edge of the county's
development front, which means larger lots, newer construction, and a lower
density than anywhere else on this list. The town has been deliberate about
maintaining that character as it grows.

It fits buyers who have already lived in a Frisco or Plano subdivision and
decided they want more land, and buyers whose budget clears the entry point
comfortably.

## Schools

Prosper ISD is among the fastest-growing districts in the state and has been
opening campuses on a rapid schedule. That growth is generally a positive —
newer facilities, smaller established campuses — but it also means attendance
boundaries move. Ask directly about anticipated rezoning for any address you are
serious about.

Note that Prosper ISD extends beyond the town limits and serves portions of
neighboring cities, while some Prosper addresses fall into other districts.

## What buyers get wrong about Prosper

The commute is the trade, and it is a real one. Buyers who work in the Legacy
corridor or further south should drive the route at rush hour before committing.
The lot size is genuine and the schools are genuine; the distance is also genuine.

Second point: infrastructure is still catching up to the growth in places.
Retail, medical, and road capacity in a given area may be planned rather than
built. Ask what exists today versus what is announced.
