## Who Plano fits

Plano is the grown-up of the group. It built out decades before its neighbors,
which means mature trees, established neighborhoods, and homes that have already
proven how they age. If you want a yard with shade in year one rather than year
fifteen, this is the city.

It also puts you closest to the Legacy and Granite Park employment corridor,
which matters more than people expect once a commute becomes daily rather than
theoretical.

## Schools

Plano ISD. The district uses a distinctive structure where high schools are split
into ninth-grade centers, senior high campuses, and separate senior campuses, so
the school your child attends changes as they progress. Attendance zones do not
always follow the boundaries people assume from a map — verify the specific
address rather than the neighborhood name.

## What buyers get wrong about Plano

Buyers coming from Frisco or Prosper often assume Plano means older and therefore
cheaper. West Plano frequently prices above newer construction further north,
because lot size, tree cover, and location are things a new build cannot
manufacture. If you want new finishes at a lower price, look north. If you want
land and location, Plano is where the money goes.
