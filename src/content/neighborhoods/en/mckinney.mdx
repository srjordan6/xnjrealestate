## Who McKinney fits

McKinney is the county seat, and unlike its neighbors it has an actual historic
downtown square — buildings that predate the suburb, not a retail development
designed to resemble one. That gives the city a center of gravity the others
manufacture at considerable expense.

The result is a split personality in the best sense: a walkable historic core on
the east side, and newer master-planned development to the west. Buyers who find
Frisco too uniform often land here.

## What homes cost

McKinney generally delivers more square footage per dollar than Frisco or
Prosper. Homes near the downtown square carry a character premium that newer
construction west of US-75 does not.

## Schools

McKinney ISD. The district serves most of the city, but portions of McKinney fall
into Frisco ISD, Prosper ISD, or Allen ISD depending on the address. This is the
single most common surprise for buyers here — a McKinney mailing address does not
guarantee a McKinney ISD school. Verify at the address level, always.

## What buyers get wrong about McKinney

The commute. McKinney sits farther north than Plano or Allen, and buyers pricing
in a Legacy-area job sometimes discover that US-75 at rush hour is a materially
different experience than the map suggests. Drive it at the hour you would
actually drive it before you commit.
