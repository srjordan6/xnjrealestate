## Who Allen fits

Allen is the most cohesive city on this list. It is served by a single school
district with a single high school, which produces something the larger suburbs
cannot replicate: a city where families across every neighborhood end up in the
same place. Whether that appeals to you is personal, but it is the defining
characteristic.

It also sits between Plano and McKinney on US-75, which makes it a practical
compromise for households commuting in different directions.

## Schools

Allen ISD. One high school serves the entire city, so unlike Frisco, Plano, or
McKinney, the choice of neighborhood does not determine the high school. That
removes a variable that drives pricing everywhere else in the county — elementary
zoning still varies, but the high-school question does not apply.

## What buyers get wrong about Allen

Inventory. Buyers assume they can take their time, then find that the home they
liked went under contract in days. Allen's housing stock is largely built out, so
supply depends on people choosing to move rather than on new construction. Be
ready to act, and have financing arranged before you start looking.
